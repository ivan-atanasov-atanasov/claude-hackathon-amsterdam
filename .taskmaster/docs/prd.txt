AMSTERDAM CLIMATE RISK DASHBOARD - PRD
======================================
Working title: KlimaatKaart
Hackathon: Whale x Anthropic Claude Code - Amsterdam (April 25-26, 2026)

OVERVIEW
--------
An integrated climate risk dashboard for Amsterdam city planners that combines
heat stress, flood risk, drought vulnerability, and vulnerable population data
into a single map-based interface. Enables data-driven prioritization of climate
adaptation investments by neighborhood.

PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------
Amsterdam city planners need to prioritize climate adaptation investments across
100+ neighborhoods, but critical data is fragmented:

- Heat stress maps: Klimaateffectatlas (separate system)
- Flood/drought risk: maps.amsterdam.nl (separate GeoJSON layers)
- Vulnerable population: GGD/CBS (separate demographic databases)
- Municipal heat response: Amsterdams Hitteplan (PDF protocols)

Planners cannot easily answer: "Which neighborhoods should we prioritize for
intervention, considering compound climate AND social vulnerability risk?"

CLIMATE CONTEXT (KNMI'14 Projections for 2050)
----------------------------------------------
- Summer temperature: +1 to +2.3C (more heat waves)
- Winter precipitation: +3 to +17% (sewer overload)
- Sea level rise: +15 to +40 cm (flood risk)
- Extreme rainfall: More intense (flash flooding)
- Summer drought: Longer dry periods

VULNERABLE POPULATIONS (from Hitteplan)
---------------------------------------
- Elderly (65+), especially living alone
- Chronically ill
- Young children
- Homeless (dak- en thuislozen)
- Outdoor workers
- People in poverty
- People with small social networks

TARGET USER
-----------
Municipal Climate Adaptation Planner at Gemeente Amsterdam

Key need: "Show me which neighborhoods have the highest COMPOUND risk
(heat + flood + vulnerable population) so I can defend my budget allocation."

MVP FEATURES (P0 - Must Have)
-----------------------------
1. INTERACTIVE MAP WITH TOGGLEABLE LAYERS
   - Amsterdam neighborhood boundaries
   - Toggle: Heat stress | Flood risk | Drought | Vulnerable population
   - Visual overlay for compound risk intensity
   - Source: WMS tiles from Klimaateffectatlas + Amsterdam GeoJSON

2. NEIGHBORHOOD RISK RANKING TABLE
   - Sortable list of all neighborhoods
   - Columns: Name | Heat | Flood | Vulnerability | Combined score
   - Click row to zoom to neighborhood on map

3. NEIGHBORHOOD DETAIL PANEL
   - Click neighborhood -> side panel with:
   - Individual risk scores breakdown
   - Key statistics (population, % elderly, housing age)
   - Risk trend indicator for 2050

4. SCENARIO TOGGLE
   - Switch between "Current" and "2050 Projection"
   - Visual indication of risk changes

NICE TO HAVE (P1)
-----------------
5. Risk threshold alerts (highlight neighborhoods > threshold)
6. Export/share functionality

TECHNICAL STACK
---------------
Frontend: Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS 4
Map: Leaflet + react-leaflet (or Mapbox GL JS)
Backend: FastAPI (Python 3.14)
Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
External: WMS from Klimaateffectatlas
Deploy: Vercel (frontend) + Railway (backend)

------------
| Layer                  | Source              | Format  |
|------------------------|---------------------|---------|
| Heat stress            | Klimaateffectatlas  | WMS     |
| Urban Heat Island      | Atlas Leefomgeving  | WMS     |
| Flood risk             | Klimaateffectatlas  | WMS     |
| Drought risk           | Klimaateffectatlas  | WMS     |

API ENDPOINTS
-------------
GET  /health
GET  /api/neighborhoods
GET  /api/neighborhoods/:id
GET  /api/neighborhoods/:id/risks
GET  /api/rankings?sort_by=combined
GET  /api/scenarios
POST /api/rankings/calculate

DATABASE SCHEMA
---------------
neighborhoods:
  - id, code, name, district, geometry (GeoJSON), population, area_km2

risk_scores:
  - id, neighborhood_id, scenario, heat_score, flood_score,
    drought_score, vulnerability_score, combined_score

vulnerability_factors:
  - id, neighborhood_id, elderly_pct, low_income_pct,
    single_person_households_pct, poor_housing_pct, green_space_m2_per_capita

USER STORIES
------------
US1: View compound risk map - see heat, flood, vulnerability on one map
US2: Rank neighborhoods by risk - sortable table with combined scores
US3: Inspect neighborhood details - click for risk breakdown
US4: Compare current vs future - toggle 2050 projections

SUCCESS CRITERIA
----------------
Demo "wow moments":
1. "Finally, one map" - all layers on single view
2. "I can rank neighborhoods" - sorted priority list
3. "2050 is scary" - future scenario shows risk increase
4. "Click and understand" - detail panel explains WHY

Technical:
- Map loads < 3 seconds
- All 100+ neighborhoods with scores
- 4 layers toggleable
- Current vs 2050 toggle works
- No critical bugs in demo

COMPOUND RISK FORMULA (Proposed)
--------------------------------
combined_score = (
  0.35 * heat_score +
  0.25 * flood_score +
  0.15 * drought_score +
  0.25 * vulnerability_score
)
