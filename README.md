# claude-hackathon-amsterdam

Whale x Anthropic: Claude Code Hackaton - Amsterdam

​Build Local: A 24-hour hackathon in Amsterdam, co-hosted with Anthropic

​Technology is getting rapidly more powerful — but not everyone has equal access.

​On April 25–26, we are bringing together 100 of the best builders to create products for people and sectors that have less access, or risk being left behind entirely.

​Build local: think minorities, non-profits, municipalities, emergency services, or even countries.

​In 24 hours, you will go from idea to product, and pitch in front of the Jury and investors. And yes, we have crazy prizes worth over 10k+ 🏆🚀
